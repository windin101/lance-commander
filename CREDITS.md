Adapted from **"Mad Dog Prime, aka Vulture for Battletech"** by **Syllogy**
(https://www.printables.com/model/1863972-mad-dog-prime-aka-vulture-for-battletech-syllogy), licensed
under **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). Changed: scaled to game
size, cut into parts, re-posed to a standing pose, joint caps added, painted and rigged to the game's
skeleton. The adapted mesh is licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Not endorsed by Syllogy. Full notice:
`game/assets/mechs/vulture_hd.LICENSE.md`.

Other HD 'Mech, vehicle and building models are AI-generated derivatives of the Shared Source art
