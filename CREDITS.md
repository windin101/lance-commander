## Third-party assets

### Adapted HD models (mechs, vehicles, copters)

These HD meshes are adaptations of 3D-printable miniatures shared under Creative Commons licences by their
creators (Syllogy, UnrestedGuy, RGriffin65, Shiaic3D, Pfelpensson, PrysmaTheMagical, MiniHulkMechs, ChewiewookTheVindicator, maggiweber and smight). Each was scaled to
game size, cut into or put together from its parts, fitted to the MechCommander 2 model it replaces, painted and
rigged to that model's skeleton or parts. Each adapted mesh is shared under the licence of its original, listed
below. None of the creators has endorsed this project. The full notice for each, with the changes made, is in
`game/assets/<mechs|vehicles>/<asset>_hd.LICENSE.md`.
