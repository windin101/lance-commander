# lifeboatrusted and lifeboatcrashed (v3 objects) - licence and attribution

The meshes of `lifeboatrusted*.glb` and `lifeboatcrashed*.glb` (intact, `_damaged` and `_destroyed`, both detail levels) are adaptations of:

> **"NASA's X-38 CRV"** by **cryptojones**, https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3453862
> licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

## Changes made

They are built by `tools/art/v3/bld_lifeboat.py` from the already adapted sculpt `game/assets/objects/lifeboat_hd.glb` (see `lifeboat_hd.LICENSE.md`
for the first set of changes). Further changes: the hull is welded and re-posed (rolled onto a wing for the rusted one; nose-down with a crumpled
nose, a sheared-off wing, a torn-open dorsal skin and a new interior and ribs for the crashed one), gear removed or sheared, vertex-coloured for rust,
grime and burn, simplified by vertex clustering for the distant detail level, and for the destroyed states cut into chunks lying on a new charred slab.
Paint is the project's own `boatrust` material set.
