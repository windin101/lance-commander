## Changes made

Made by `tools/art/v3/v3_mech.py` with the settings in `tools/art/v3/hunter.json` (Pipeline A, 2026-10-24): the printed
sculpt was scaled to game size; split into rigid parts on the kit's own part lines (crease-bounded panels);
re-posed part by part from the sculpt's pose into the MechCommander 2 rest pose; modelled joint actuators (drums and
balls) and flat decks added where parts meet; reduced to game density; given a new UV atlas; baked (normal, ambient
occlusion, edges) from the full-detail sculpt in Blender Cycles; and painted with a hand-authored zone mask and baked
wear (chips, grime, dust, soot). The textures are original work made from those bakes.

## Notes on the source

Vehicle rework (LANCE-18, 2026-10-30), differs from the mech text above: no joints or actuators. The kit's fused hull was split into the hull (with the road wheels, which stay fixed), the turret (`Hunter_Turret`, the kit's mounted turret and LRM rack, a rigid part that turns about the vertical axis) and two track belts (`track_l`, `track_r`, found geometrically as the layer of link blocks under the side silhouette by `tools/art/v3/veh_prep.py (hunter_prep.json)`). The sculpt was welded at 2 mm and planar-dissolved at 8 degrees before reduction (`veh_lowpoly.py`). The belts' UVs were laid out by `veh_lowpoly.py` as a repeating tread strip (two link pitches per period) so the game can scroll it; the hull and turret use an xatlas atlas. Revision 2 (2026-10-30): the kit's wheels are only fused lower half discs, so the road wheels, idler and sprocket (`wheel_l0`..`wheel_r7`) are OWN GEOMETRY, generated by `tools/art/v3/veh_prep.py (hunter_prep.json)` (`make_wheel`: tyre drum, recessed rim, hub boss, bolts) on axle centres and radii fitted to the kit's wheel arcs; the fused half discs were cut from the hull. This own geometry is part of the CC BY-SA 4.0 derivative. The turret is reduced by welding and planar dissolve only (no collapse).
