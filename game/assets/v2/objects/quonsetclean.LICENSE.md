# quonsetclean.glb (v2), licence

Rebuilt in SolidCraft from the dimensions of the MechCommander 2 Shared Source model (game/assets/objects/quonsetclean.glb); its geometry therefore stays under that release's licence (docs/MC2-SOURCE-EULA.md): non-commercial use only. The surface textures (game/assets/v2/shared/) and the AO bake are new work made by tools/art/hd_v2_textures.py and tools/art/hd_v2_building.py.
