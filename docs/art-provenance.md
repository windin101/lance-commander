# Art provenance

## HD mechs (`game/assets/mechs/<mech>_hd.glb`, `<mech>_hd_paint.bin`)

These are generated by `tools/art/hd_mech.py` on a local ComfyUI. They are AI derivatives of the
Shared Source release's mech models and textures, so they carry that release's EULA terms:
**non-commercial use only**.

### Pipeline

1. **Render:** the original model in a neutral paint scheme, at a three-quarter view (yaw 35, padded on white).
2. **Concept:** a restyle with Qwen-Image-Edit 2509 fp8 at 512 px. The prompt is in `tools/art/hd_concept.py`.
3. **Mesh:**
   - Hunyuan3D 2.1, using ComfyUI's native nodes and the "Image to Model" blueprint settings.
   - DecimateMesh (target 20k), MeshSmoothNormals (50°) and UnwrapMesh.
   - Normal map and ambient occlusion baked from the high-poly mesh at 2048.
4. **Rig** (`tools/art/hd_rig.py`):
   - Fit to the original model: yaw search, then rigid ICP.
   - Rigid skinning to the original joints, with majority smoothing.
   - The original paint mask is transferred by nearest-surface lookup. MechPaint team colours still apply, and the original's 20 animations are kept unchanged.
   - Small disconnected fragments (generation debris) are dropped. Vertices are welded by position first, so UV seams don't split the mesh into "fragments".
5. **Wrecks:** an HD mech has no separate wreck model. When destroyed it holds the last frame of FallForward with a scorched material (`MechView._scorch`).

paint. Made by `tools/art/stl_rig_vulture.py`. The adapted mesh is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; the skeleton and animations in the
same file stay under the Shared Source licence. Full notice: `game/assets/mechs/vulture_hd.LICENSE.md`.

### Per building (`--kind objects`, `game/assets/objects/<name>_hd.glb`)

Buildings are static, so the pipeline skips skinning. The original colour texture (`<name>_0.png`) is transferred onto the HD mesh and embedded in the .glb.

The building-specific steps are:

- The mesh uses Hunyuan's `basic` voxel mesher. Surface nets break on large flat walls.
- The fit scales by footprint, then matches the bounds per axis. The bounds are surface percentiles, so thin masts don't count.
- The transfer samples the original by area and leaves out the lit-window overlay meshes.
- The result is tone-matched to the original's average colour.

When destroyed, a building swaps to the original `_dam` model.

### Environment notes (workstation, 2026-10-19)

- **Attention mode:** run the ComfyUI used for the Qwen edits with `--use-split-cross-attention`. With PyTorch SDPA on this ROCm nightly, edits intermittently come back as garbage.
- **Retries:** split attention still fails now and then, so `hd_mech.py` validates each concept and retries with the next seed.

**v2.1 (2026-10-23, after Lee's review).**
- **Joints.** Each joint gets a visible housing: a ball, or a drum about the joint's own animated axis. Both parts are
  clipped exactly onto it in the game's rest frame, where the skeleton's pivots are. The pelvis is squared to the
  hips (`square_pelvis`: the Bushwacker sculpt's pelvis was yawed 32°, which put its hip sockets 497 mm from the
  thighs'), and each leg keeps its own bone lengths. Where the print fused two moving parts outside a housing
  (plates resting on a thigh or a skirt), the cut is closed with an ear-clipped cap.
- **Maps per unit.** `mask` (2048 px): highlights, metal tone and glass codes (canopy, lens, light). `normal`
  (2048). `detail` (1024): AO, curvature, numbers and crest, soot by weapons and vents. `zones` (1024): body
  region, inset piping, leg stripe and limb band, for region schemes. `camo` (512): three octaves of noise at the
  rest-pose position, so a pattern stays fixed on a moving part. That makes 14.3 MB per mech at BPTC with mipmaps,
  inside the 16 MB budget.
- **Paint schemes.** These are `game/data/faction_paint.json`, generated by `tools/art/faction_paint.py`.
  - The player's scheme lives in `user://settings.cfg [paint]`. The default is grey #9a9a9a with red #d02e11
    piping and bands. It applies to every player-owned unit (player team, commander 0) in missions, and to the bay,
    lab and market models. Its patterns are plain, woodland, snow and urban.
  - Four faction schemes follow Lee's reference palettes in `/mnt/nvme1n1p1/mc2-rework/ref/ucc/`; the file notes
    which palette each follows: davion ("thorin" camouflage), steiner, liao and bandits ("arkab" camouflage).
  - A mission's colour triple maps to a faction. The mapping is verified by warrior brain names where the mission
    has them (mc2_17) and otherwise inferred from the missions whose briefings name the faction; the file records
    which.
  - Rebels and every unmapped triple keep the mission's colours on the original's team mask.
  - Crests are generic shapes (a shield), not unit insignia.
- **Canopy glass (2026-10-24).** Lee outlines each mech's panes by hand, as closed blue lines on the aligned
  sheets from `tools/art/canopy_pick.py sheet <mech>` (front, 3/4 and top, fixed orthographic cameras around the
  cockpit node). `canopy_pick.py outlines <mech> IMAGE:VIEW[:X0] ...` stores the filled outlines and the exact
  Blender cameras in `tools/art/hd_v2/<mech>.json` (`v2.canopy_outlines`). The `glass` stage of `hd_v2.py` takes
  whole planar patches where an outline covers them, and otherwise knifes the outline into the mesh (a conforming
  split, so the mesh stays watertight) and keeps the faces that a face-ID render shows inside it. Glass islands
  are kept only when chained (within 6 % of the height) to the island nearest the cockpit. Nothing is taken from
  the original's textures. Hand edits: none; the outlines are Lee's.

Source sculpts for every adapted model are staged on `/mnt/nvme1n1p1/mc2-sources/<kind>/<name>/` (zip or STL as
downloaded, plus `SOURCE.txt`; `tools/art/stage_sources.py`, index in `index.json`); they are not in the repository.

## HUD paper dolls (`game/assets/ui/dolls/<unit>_doll.png`, `dolls.json`)

These are derived from this project's own HD models (`game/assets/mechs/*_hd.glb`, `game/assets/vehicles/*_hd.glb`) by `tools/art/paper_doll.py` (2026-10-20), with per-unit overrides in `tools/art/paper_doll.json`. Each mask inherits the licence of the model it was drawn from: the Shared Source terms (non-commercial) for Hunyuan and CAD models, and the third-party licence recorded in `original/hd/<unit>/provenance.json` for adapted sculpts. Nothing is generated. Rerunning the tool rebuilds them exactly; `python3 -I tools/art/paper_doll.py <unit>` redoes one unit.

- **Format.** RGBA PNG. R = G = B = location index, A = 255 inside the silhouette; outside, A = 0. Treat alpha as the authority, because Godot's `fix_alpha_border` is set off in the sidecars but a reimport with defaults bleeds RGB. The import must stay lossless (`compress/mode=0`) with no mipmaps, or the index values break.
- **Mechs** (30, plus copters): SimMech.Body order, seen from behind in the rest pose (the pilot's view), about 256 px tall. The rear doll is the same mask, with Armor RCTORSO/RLTORSO/RRTORSO on locations 1/2/3.
- **Vehicles, infantry, powered armour:** SimVehicle.Section order, plan view with forward up. Hull thirds are front and rear, and the middle third is split left and right. The turret is drawn inside a hull rim, so no section is hidden.
- **`poweredarmor_mech_doll.png`:** an extra 8-location doll for powered armour. In the sim, powered armour is a 5-section SimVehicle.
- **Overrides** (see the `_why` in the JSON): catapult (the LRM launchers are the arms), flea (the side pods are the arms), raven (no right-arm joint: the outer end of the right torso wing), uller (narrower centre torso), heavycopter (drawn bigger), poweredarmor_mech (the left arm is skinned to the torso).
- **Review sheet:** `swarm/gallery/paper_dolls.png`.

## Weapon and damage effects (`game/assets/fx/`, `game/data/weapon_fx.json`, I87)

Nothing here is generated by a model or taken from the original's art.

- **Sprites** (`glow.png`, `spark.png`, `smoke.png`, `fire.png`, `ring.png`, `noise.png`): drawn procedurally by `tools/art/fx_textures.py` (2026-10-23) with numpy/scipy noise seeded with `crc32(name)`, so a rerun writes the same files. New work, same terms as the rest of the project. `smoke.png` and `fire.png` are 4 x 4 sheets of 128 px puffs; fire is an intensity map that `fx_sprite.gdshader` colours with a ramp.
- **Shaders** (`game/shaders/fx_*.gdshader`): new code.
- **The look table** (`game/data/weapon_fx.json`): hand-tuned. Each weapon's `original` block is the baseline that `tools/art/weapon_fx_original.py` reads from the Shared Source: `Objects/Effects.csv` (the effect id is the row index; the gosFX trail, flare, hit and miss names) and the weapon-bolt FITs it points at through `object2.rsp` (beam duration and width, bolt length, width, speed, colours, arc, splash radius). Only numbers and names are copied, which the Shared Source terms allow; they carry its non-commercial terms. The gosFX library (`Effects/mc2.fx`) is binary and was not decoded: its effect names are recorded, its particle curves are not used.
- **Tuning from the baseline:** colours keep the original's hue at full brightness (pulse lasers `#007800` became `#1eff3c`, Clan heavy lasers `#5a5aff` became `#6a5cff`), and beam durations and pulse counts follow the FITs. Widths are about twice the original's `BulgeWidth`, and the beam shader holds a minimum on-screen width, so beams read at the 112 to 612 m RTS zoom. Arcs are higher than the original's 6 m so LRM volleys read as lobbed.
- **Review:** `game/scenes/fx_range.tscn` fires each family on a real map with the sim frozen. `tools/art/fx_review.py record OUT` records the clips (run it under the Godot lock) and `fx_review.py sheet OUT` builds the contact sheet.

## Pilot portraits (`game/assets/ui/portraits/<profile>.png`, I84)

Generated 2026-10-23 by `tools/art/portraits.py` (feature words, prompt, negatives, reseeds and crop boxes in `tools/art/portraits.json`), with Qwen-Image (`qwen_image_fp8_e4m3fn`, Apache-2.0 weights) on the local ComfyUI. Text-to-image only: no reference image, so no likeness is copied. They are new work under the project's own terms (non-commercial with the rest of the remake). `pilot_portraits.gd` loads them by profile name and falls back to the silhouette.

- **Method.** 1248x1472 draw, 30 steps, euler/simple, cfg 2.5, shift 3.1, seed `crc32(profile) + reseed`. Then a crop that puts the face at 40 % of the crop width (OpenCV Haar box, stored per `<profile>_s<seed>` in the JSON so a rerun needs no detector), resized to 660x920 RGB (33:46, the portrait well). `python3 tools/art/portraits.py --only <profile>` redraws one; `--sheet out.png` builds the contact sheet. Raw draws are kept outside the repo in `/mnt/nvme1n1p1/claude-scratch/portraits/raw/`.
- **What came from the original.** Only the apparent gender and rough age of each pilot, read from the original pilot photos (`Source/Data/Art/MCUI_High2.tga`, 33x46 cells, 7 per row, `PictureIndex`) and the voice sets (`Source/Data/Sound/Pilots/Pilot<N>`, where `pilotA`..`pilotz` = Pilot1..Pilot26; folder and `.pak` sizes match). The photos were looked at, not used as input. Ethnicity, hair, build and styling were chosen fresh to suit each callsign.
- **Voice check.** `alefiury/wav2vec2-large-xlsr-53-gender-recognition-librispeech` (Hugging Face) scored 16 lines per voice set. The table gives the mean P(female). It agrees with the photo for 24 of 26 voices. Rooster (0.51 over all 39 lines) and Longshot (0.60 over 37) are split, with confident answers per file, so those two follow the photo until someone listens.
- **Hand edits:** none.
