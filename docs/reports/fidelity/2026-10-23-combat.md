# Combat and AI fidelity check, 2026-10-23

**Question:** do our vehicles and enemy units hit harder, more often or faster than the original's, or did the
original really play the mass-enemy missions (mc2_15, 19, 20, 22, 23) and mc2_17's betrayal this hard?

**Short answer:** the numbers we copied from the original all match: weapons, armour, pilots, hit chance and damage.
Three things differ in what the game does with them:

1. **The Partisan and the Legion fire freely in our game.** In the original they get two shots per mission
   (an original bug).
2. **Our heat system** only taxes mechs, so it hits the player's lance hardest. The original had no heat.
3. **Our contact and line-of-sight model** is a placeholder.

The first explains mc2_15 completely and most of mc2_22. The second explains most of mc2_19. mc2_17 is
hard in the original too, and mc2_20 and mc2_23 are still unexplained.

The measurements use a scratch copy of the autoplay bot with two switches:
- **Original vehicle-node rule:** Partisans and Legions fire their first two weapons once, then never again.
- **Heat off:** the original has no heat.

The probe tools and raw numbers are in `docs/reports/fidelity/2026-10-23-combat/`: `make_variant.py`, `arena.gd`,
`missions.csv` and `arena.txt`. The game code was not changed.

## Checked and faithful

- **Hit chance** (`sim_combat.gd:14-67` against `mover.cpp:6197-6420`; `GroundVehicle::calcAttackChance` just calls it, `gvehicl.cpp:4025-4033`):
  - The base is Gunnery. Medium difficulty is ×100% for both sides (`gamesys.fit:51-54`), and `fix_enemy_difficulty` uses `EnemySkills[1] = 100`, so it changes nothing at Medium.
  - Range bands with the ±12.07 m cell-diagonal tolerance, and 100% against non-movers and disabled targets.
  - Target and weapon specialties and aimed shots; clamped to 5-95.
  - There is **no** movement, target-size or range modifier in the original either. `AttackerMoveModifier` and `TargetMoveModifierTable` only feed BR (`gvehicl.cpp:1304-1306`).
- **Damage, recycle, heat and range:** all 37 weapons in `components.json` match `compbas.csv`, and so does ammo per ton.
  - Missiles: `ammoAmount` is always 1 (`cmponent.cpp:92,232-238`), so a salvo is one hit of the CSV damage, in the original's vehicle and mech paths alike (`gvehicl.cpp:4646-4668`, `mech.cpp:8051-8060`).
  - Ammo: spent on a hit; on a miss only for non-streak weapons.
- **Vehicle armour, internal structure, speed, turret yaw and inventory:** all 28 `game/data/vehicles/*.json` match their `Data/Objects/*.fit` (Legion = `TankK`).
  - Hit location: turret on (d100+d100)/2 ≥ 70, i.e. 17.7%, otherwise by bearing (`sim_vehicle.gd:79-90` = `gvehicl.cpp:4037-4071`).
  - Damage: armour, then internal structure 1, and the section dies (`sim_vehicle.gd:94-108` = `gvehicl.cpp:4289-4368`).
- **Enemy pilots:** `pilots.json` matches `Warriors/PMW*.FIT`: Green 30, Regular 40, Veteran 55 and Elite 65 gunnery, piloting 5.
- **Head hits and wounds:** a head hit of ≥2 damage gives 1 wound, and 6 wounds kill (`mech.cpp:7537-7538`, `warrior.cpp:1927-1966`). Each AC5 head hit (2.0) wounds, in both games.
- **Help requests:** `requestHelp` and `calcAttackPlan` spread helpers over the enemies by threat ratio (`ablmc2.cpp:212-250,5993-6029`); ours does the same by tonnage (`sim_abl.gd:1310-1345`). Neither focus-fires.
  - Small difference: the original takes helpers from any commander on a non-player team; ours from the caller's commander only. In these missions every enemy is commander 1.
- **Reinforcements and spawns:** none of 15, 19, 20, 22 and 23 creates units. Everything exists from the start, and waves are brains waiting on script flags and timers, which our ABL runtime runs from the original files (no ABL errors in the sweep).
- **Player-favouring rules in the original:**
  - The range-specialist bug C2 gives ShortRangeSpecialist +15 everywhere, but no campaign pilot in `pilots.json` has a range specialty, so it has no effect.
  - Toughness only affects surviving an ejection (`warrior.cpp:1190`).
  - Nothing else gives player pilots a hidden bonus at Medium.
  - The original's real player-side advantages are F1 to F5.

## mc2_17: was the player protected in the original?

No. After the cinematic, `BothRunAways` starts a timer: a video plays 5 s later and `liaoBetrays` fires 5 s
after that (`mc2_17.abl:810-826`). There is no `setNoPain`, invulnerability or delay.

- **Renard's Atlas** leaves the map (`mc2_17_EC1_Steiner_Boss.abl:226-237`, `objectRemove`).
- **The Centre group** (2 Zeus, Highlander, Bushwacker) patrols to its base. Its first waypoint, (-934, -542) m, is
  30 m from the player's start at (-909, -559) m, so it walks onto the lance with an attack-on-contact state
  (`mc2_17_ec1_cen_mech_0.abl:62-70,214-223`).
- **The Flank mechs** (2 Hollander, 3 Werewolf) run `FlankPlayer`.
- **The two Highlander guards** (Elite 65, Veteran 55) guard the hill at (-1131, -260) m. They only leave the map
  after Liao's son is attacked (`mc2_17_ec1_stein_guard_1.abl:124-133,216-251`).

Skills and start positions come straight from the FIT (Steiner force 200-400 m from the drop). Heat makes no
difference: off, the lance still dies at 178-203 s (vs 183-194). This is the original's design ("Don't Die" is
the mission's tagline). F3 and F4 could only matter at the edges, because the patrol walks right through the
lance.

## Verdicts

- **mc2_15: port bug (F1).** The 9 Legions fire freely. With the original's rule the same bot wins every seed.
- **mc2_22: port bug (F1), plus F2.** The Partisans are the whole early-damage story. With the original's rule
  they barely fire and the lance reaches the Elite Guard: 1/3 wins, 2/3 with heat off too. F3 (the EC1 group sees
  the drop at 405-492 m) probably accounts for some of the rest.
- **mc2_19: mostly port (F2).** The heat tax turns 2/3 wins into 0/3. The rest is the "destroy 1/3 of 82 units" scale.
- **mc2_17: the original is genuinely hard.** It is a designed betrayal with no protection, skills and positions as
  in the original, and heat makes no difference. Still a balance call for Lee (I24).
- **mc2_20: unknown.** No Partisans or Legions, and heat off doesn't fix it. The candidates left are F3, F4 and F5
  (the patrols converging). Probe: let `SENSOR_M` and a visual-only LOS radius be set from outside (they are
  constants today), then rerun the 3 seeds with 225 m.
- **mc2_23: unknown, leaning balance.** Neither switch wins a seed, though heat off lasts longer (up to 1098 s, 56
  kills). The same probe as mc2_20 would settle F3/F5. Otherwise this is the I29 balance call.
